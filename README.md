# Wabi

Wabi is an Android app from Kenya that finds the people who do jobs near you, such as plumbers, electricians, mechanics, cleaners, painters, carpenters, boda riders and movers.

Website: https://wabi.co.ke

## How it works

- You see the workers who are near you and call one from the app.
- You watch the worker come to you on a map.
- You pay with M-Pesa before the work starts, and Wabi holds the money.
- The worker is paid to their M-Pesa when you confirm the job is done. Nobody has to chase anyone.

## For workers

Add your skill and photos of your work, and the people near you who need it can find you. There is no fee to join. Wabi keeps 7 percent of each paid job and the rest goes to your M-Pesa.

## Where

Kenya. You need a Kenyan phone number and M-Pesa. Android only for now.
